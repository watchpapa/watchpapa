## 1. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** means all non-public, proprietary information disclosed by one Party (the **"Disclosing Party"**) to the other Party (the **"Receiving Party"**) in connection with discussions, collaboration, or access to watchpapa services. This includes but is not limited to:

- Source code, algorithms, and technical architecture
- Database schemas, API specifications, and design documents
- Business plans, financial data, and user analytics
- Product roadmaps and unreleased features
- User data, customer lists, and account information
- Security measures, authentication mechanisms, and encryption keys
- Marketing strategies, pricing models, and sales projections
- Any other information marked as "Confidential" or reasonably understood to be confidential

### 1.2 Exclusions

Confidential Information does **NOT** include information that:

- Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party
- Was rightfully possessed by the Receiving Party prior to disclosure, as evidenced by written records
- Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without confidentiality obligations
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records
- Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (see Section 3 for notification requirements)

---

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees to:

### 2.1 Protect Confidential Information

Maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence using reasonable care (not less than the care used for its own confidential information, but in no case less than reasonable care).

### 2.2 Limit Access

- Restrict access to Confidential Information to employees, contractors, and advisors who have a legitimate need to know
- Ensure those individuals are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement
- Remain responsible for any breach by those individuals

### 2.3 Permitted Use

Use Confidential Information **solely** for:
- Evaluating a potential business relationship with the Disclosing Party
- Performing services or deliverables under a separate written agreement
- Internal research and analysis related to the intended purpose

**Prohibited:** Using Confidential Information to:
- Develop competing products or services
- Disclose to competitors
- Reverse-engineer or attempt to derive trade secrets
- Sell or license to third parties without prior written consent

### 2.4 No License

Disclosure of Confidential Information grants **no license or rights** to the Receiving Party except as expressly stated in a separate written agreement.

---

## 3. REQUIRED DISCLOSURES

### 3.1 Legal Compulsion

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, subpoena, or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:

1. **Promptly notify** the Disclosing Party in writing (unless legally prohibited from doing so)
2. **Provide reasonable notice** to allow the Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or injunction
3. **Disclose only the minimum** information legally required
4. **Cooperate** with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain protection (e.g., seal the disclosure, mark as confidential)

### 3.2 Exception

The Receiving Party need not provide notice if disclosure is to law enforcement in an emergency or if providing notice would undermine legitimate government or law enforcement investigations.

---

## 4. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

Upon request by the Disclosing Party, or upon termination/expiration of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall:

- **Return or certify destruction** of all Confidential Information (in tangible form: documents, media, devices)
- **Delete or overwrite** electronic copies of Confidential Information from all systems and backups
- **Retain only one copy** (if required by law or regulation), which remains subject to confidentiality obligations
- Provide written certification of completion within 30 days

---

## 5. NO LICENSE OR OBLIGATION

### 5.1 No License

Disclosure of Confidential Information creates **no license, right, or obligation** to:
- Buy, sell, or license the Confidential Information
- Enter into any business relationship
- Support, maintain, or develop the Confidential Information

### 5.2 Evaluation

Either Party may evaluate the Confidential Information and terminate discussions at any time without obligation. No waiver of this Agreement occurs by requesting or accepting Confidential Information.

---

## 6. TERM & TERMINATION

### 6.1 Effective Date

This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date above.

### 6.2 Duration

- **Confidential Information disclosed in tangible form (documents, recordings):** Remain confidential for **3 years** after disclosure
- **Trade secrets or sensitive security information:** Remain confidential for **5 years** after disclosure (or as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law)
- **User data (GDPR-regulated):** Protected under GDPR and related data protection laws indefinitely

### 6.3 Survival

Confidentiality obligations **survive** termination of any business relationship between the Parties.

### 6.4 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement with **written notice**. Upon termination, the Receiving Party's obligations under Sections 2 and 4 continue for the durations specified above.

## 8. REMEDIES

### 8.1 Equitable Relief

The Receiving Party acknowledges that breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are insufficient. The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek:

- Injunctive relief (court order to stop unauthorized use/disclosure)
- Specific performance
- Any other equitable remedies available under applicable law

This does **not** limit the Disclosing Party's right to recover monetary damages or pursue other remedies.

### 8.2 Prevailing Party Fees

If the Disclosing Party prevails in any legal action to enforce this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall reimburse the Disclosing Party's reasonable attorney fees, court costs, and other litigation expenses.

## 9. NO OBLIGATION TO DISCLOSE

Neither Party is obligated to disclose any Confidential Information or enter into negotiations. If either Party chooses to disclose Confidential Information, that disclosure does not obligate continuation of discussions or any business relationship.
