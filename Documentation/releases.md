## v1.1

### ⭐ Ratings, Profiles, Watchlists & more

A big update just landed. Here's everything that's new:

❤️ Rate anything

You can now rate movies, shows, seasons, and individual episodes on a 1–10 heart scale. Ratings live in the sidebar of every detail page — always visible, just hover and click. Half-heart precision, full-heart display.

Every detail page also shows a community rating histogram once 10 or more users have rated it — see the full spread of scores at a glance.

👤 User profiles

Every account now has a public profile at /u/username. Your profile shows:

A bio (set it in Edit Profile)

Up to 5 pinned favourite movies or shows

Your full ratings grid

Stats — distribution, top genres, decades, and monthly activity

Click the profile circle in the top-right to go to your profile. Hover it on desktop to open the menu without navigating away.

📊 Stats (tier-gated)

Your profile stats unlock in layers based on your plan:

Everyone: total ratings, average score, your personal rating distribution

Premium+: top genres by rating count

Pro+: decade breakdown and monthly rating activity

Locked stats show a blurred preview so you can see what's waiting for you.

📋 Watchlists

Organise what you want to watch into named lists. Free users get 1 list, Premium gets 3, and Pro gets 10. Add movies and shows from their detail pages via the bookmark button. Mark items watched or unwatched as you go.

All your lists are one click away at /watchlists — each list is a tab, no extra navigation needed.

📌 Follows page

A dedicated /follows page now shows everything you're following — shows and movies in separate tabs — with unfollow buttons in one place. The "Manage follows" button in the release calendar now links here instead of opening a modal.

If your subscription downgrades and you're over your follow limit, the release calendar is blocked until you trim your list.

📅 Calendar improvements

Today's releases are highlighted in amber so they stand out immediately

Multiple episodes from the same season on the same day collapse into a single "Season X" row

Hover to zoom — hovering a day on desktop magnifies it and its neighbouring columns, dock-style

## v1.3

### 👀 Observe people, activity feed & more

Social is here — follow what your friends watch without mixing it up with title follows on the release calendar. Here's everything new since v1.2:

👀 Observe (not the same as Follow)

You can now **observe** other watchpapa users — a one-way connection to see their activity and ratings. Movie and show **Follow** on the Releases Radar is unchanged; that's still for tracking upcoming releases.

From any profile:

Tap **Observe** to connect (or **Unobserve** to stop)

See observer and observing counts; open lists at /u/username/observers and /u/username/observing

**Block** removes the connection both ways and hides their profile and ratings from you

🔍 Find people — /users

Search by username or display name. Send observe requests, accept or decline incoming ones, and jump straight to profiles from results.

📬 Observe requests — /observe-requests

If someone has a **private account**, observing them sends a request they must approve. Manage pending incoming and outgoing requests on one page. Link also lives in your profile menu.

🔒 Private accounts

In Settings you can turn on **Private account**. When enabled:

Your ratings, stats, and observer/observing lists are hidden from everyone except users who observe you with an **accepted** connection

Your pinned favourites stay visible

Your ratings still count toward anonymous community histograms on detail pages — others see the aggregate, not your name

🔔 In-app notifications

A bell in the top bar shows unread count and a quick preview dropdown. Full history at /notifications. You'll get notified for new observe requests, accepts, and when someone you observe rates something.

📰 Activity feed — /feed

A timeline of ratings from people you observe — see what your circle watched and how they scored it, newest first.

⭐ Observed ratings on detail pages

On movie, show, season, and episode pages, the sidebar includes a foldable **Observed ratings** panel: scores from people you observe who rated that title. Collapse it to a count-only header when you just want the number.

🏠 Home hero banner

The home page opens with a rotating banner highlighting Ratings, Watchlists, the release Calendar, Follow, Discover, and more — a quick tour of what watchpapa does.

🧭 Cleaner top navigation

The navbar uses a roomier layout with icon shortcuts: Find People, Activity, your Watchlists, and Releases Radar — labels on hover so the bar stays uncluttered on smaller screens.

✏️ Updates editor (editors)

Posting site updates now opens a **full-page editor** with a tall writing area — no more cramped scroll inside a small modal.

📝 Small copy updates

Site metadata now says **people** instead of talent. Import and subscription help text is clearer about what each plan includes.
